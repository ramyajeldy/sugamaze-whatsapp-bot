## Cake Care & Storage

**How do I store my cake?**
These are cool cakes (whipped cream). Please store them in the refrigerator with the lid open.

**Should I remove the dowel rods before serving?**
Yes, always remove all internal dowel/support rods from tiered cakes before cutting and serving.

**Are the cake decorations edible?**
Cake decoration depends on your choice. They come in both non-edible toppers and edible toppers.

**Is it safe to serve cake to children?**
Not always. Please ensure any allergies other than egg are accounted for. We do not make 100% allergen-free cake unless specifically requested at the time of order.

**How long can strawberry cakes be kept?**
Strawberry cakes (with fresh strawberries inside or as decoration) should be eaten the same day for best quality and safety.

**Why does dark frosting taste different?**
Dark colors (black, navy, deep red) may taste slightly bitter and can temporarily stain hands or lips. Lighter alternatives are available, or dark frosting can be removed before eating.

**How long does the cake stay fresh?**
We only make cool cakes and they are meant to be consumed the same day without proper storage conditions.

**Can I freeze the cake?**
No. Cool cakes have frosting on top and are not meant to be frozen.
