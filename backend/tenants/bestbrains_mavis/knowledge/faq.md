**What is Best Brains?**
Best Brains is an academic enrichment center offering year-round programs in Math, English, Abacus, and Coding, plus additional programs like Public Speaking, Creative Writing, and Science Lab. The Mavis location serves Mississauga, Ontario.

**What ages do you accept?**
Programs are offered for children ages 3 to 14, including early learning options (Baby Genius Math, Baby Genius English, Early Learner's Program) for younger children.

**What subjects/programs do you offer?**
Math, English, Abacus, Coding, Public Speaking, Creative Writing, and Science Lab, plus test prep for EQAO, NGAT, IB, and IBT/SciTech, and seasonal Summer Camps, Aftercare, and Homework Help.

**How much does it cost?**
Monthly pricing per child: 1 subject $159, 2 subjects $279, 3 subjects $329, 4 subjects $429. Pricing for seasonal programs (camps, aftercare) isn't listed online — contact the center directly for those.

**What are your hours?**
Monday, Wednesday–Friday 4:30 PM–8:00 PM, and Saturday 10:00 AM–12:00 PM. Closed Tuesday and Sunday.

**Where are you located?**
720 Bristol Rd W, Unit #6, Mississauga, ON L5R 4A5.

Phone 647-325-0179, or email mavis@bestbrains.com.

**How do I enroll my child?**
Online registration is available through the Best Brains website, or you can book an appointment via their Outlook-based scheduling system, or contact the center directly.
