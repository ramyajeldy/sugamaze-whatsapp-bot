# Best Brains Learning Center — Mavis (Mississauga)

Best Brains is an academic enrichment franchise offering year-round programs
for children ages 3 to 14. The Mavis location is at 720 Bristol Rd W, Unit
#6, Mississauga, ON L5R 4A5, and can be reached at 647-325-0179 or
mavis@bestbrains.com.

Core programs are Math, English, Abacus, and Coding. Additional programs
include Public Speaking, Creative Writing, and Science Lab. For younger
children, Best Brains offers Baby Genius Math, Baby Genius English, and an
Early Learner's Program. The center also runs test-prep programs for EQAO,
NGAT, IB, and IBT/SciTech, and seasonal offerings including Summer Camps
(ages 3–14), Aftercare, and Homework Help.

Monthly pricing per child is tiered by number of subjects: $159 for one
subject, $279 for two, $329 for three, and $429 for four. Class hours are
Monday and Wednesday through Friday from 4:30 PM to 8:00 PM, and Saturday
from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM; the center is closed Tuesday and Sunday.

Enrollment happens through online registration on the Best Brains website,
by booking an appointment through their Outlook-based scheduling system, or
by contacting the center directly. Best Brains operates as a franchise, with
Mavis being one of several Canadian locations.
